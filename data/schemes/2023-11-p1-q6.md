6. base case $n=1$: $5^2 - 2^3 = 25 - 8 = 17$ so true for $n=1$

assume true for $n=k$ ie $5^{2k} - 2^{3k} = 17s$ for $s \in \mathbb{Z}$ OR $5^{2k} - 2^{3k}$ is divisible by 17

Note: The assumption of truth must be clear. Do not award the $M1$ for statements such as “let $n=k$” or “$n=k$ is true”. Subsequent marks can still be awarded.

## ## EITHER

consider $n=k+1$ :
$5^{2(k+1)}-2^{3(k+1)}$
$=\left(5^{2}\right)5^{2k}-\left(2^{3}\right)2^{3k}$
$=\left(25\right)5^{2k}-\left(8\right)2^{3k}$
$=\left(17\right)5^{2k}+\left(8\right)5^{2k}-\left(8\right)2^{3k}$ OR $(25)5^{2k}-\left(25\right)2^{3k}+\left(17\right)2^{3k}$
$=\left(17\right)5^{2k}+8\left(5^{2k}-2^{3k}\right)$ OR $25\left(5^{2k}-2^{3k}\right)+\left(17\right)2^{3k}$
$=\left(17\right)5^{2k}+8\left(17s\right)$ OR $25\left(17s\right)+\left(17\right)2^{3k}$
$=\left(5^{2k}+2^{3k}\right)$ OR $17\left(25s+2^{3k}\right)$ which is divisible by 17
OR
$\left(5^{2k}-2^{3k}\right)\times5^{2}=5^{2k+2}-25\times2^{3k}=17s\times25$
$=\left(5^{2k+2}-8\times2^{3k}-17\times2^{3k}\right)=17s\times25$
$=\left(5^{2k+2}-2^{3k+3}-17\times2^{3k}\right)=17s\times25$
$=\left(5^{2(k+1)}-2^{3(k+1)}\right)=17\times2^{3k}=17s\times25$
$=\left(5^{2(k+1)}-2^{3(k+1)}\right)=17s\times25+17\times2^{3k}$
hence for $n=k+1:5^{2(k+1)}-2^{3(k+1)}=17\left(25s+2^{3k}\right)$ is divisible by 17

## ## THEN

since true for n = 1, and true for n = k implies true for n = k + 1.

therefore true for all  $ n \in \mathbb{Z}^+ $
