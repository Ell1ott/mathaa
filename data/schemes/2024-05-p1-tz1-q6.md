attempt to use integration by substitution

$$ u=\cos\left(x^{2}\right)\Longrightarrow\frac{\mathrm{d}u}{\mathrm{d}x}=-2x\sin\left(x^{2}\right) $$

$$ u=\cos\left(x^{2}\right) $$

$$ =-\frac{\pi}{2}\int_{-\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}}^{-1}\mathrm{d}u $$

$$ =\left[-\frac{\pi}{2}u\right]_{-\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}}^{-1} $$

$$ -\frac{\pi}{2}u $$
