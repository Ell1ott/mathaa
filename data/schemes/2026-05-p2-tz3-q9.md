## ### 9. (a) METHOD 1

Method 1  
number of ways of arranging boys between girls  
 $ =(P_3) = 336 $ OR  $ 3! \times C_3 $ (=6×56)  
(A1)  
arranging girls = 7!  
 $ P_3 \times 7! $ (=336×5040) OR  $ 3! \times C_3 \times 7! $ (=6×56×5040)  
(A1)  
=1693440  
(A1)  
METHOD 2  
number of ways of any two boys sitting next to each other 6×9!  
this includes a duplicate of three boys sitting next to each other 8!×3!  
(A1)  
hence number of arrangements is 10! - 6×9! + 8! × 3!  
=1693440  
(A1)  
METHOD 3  
number of ways of arranging 7 girls and a boy 8!  
number of ways of arranging remaining two boys 7×6  
hence number of arrangements is 8!×7×6 (=40320×56)  
(A1)  
=1693440  
(A1)
