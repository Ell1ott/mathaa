## ### 5. METHOD 1

use of  $ |\boldsymbol{a} \times \boldsymbol{b}| = |\boldsymbol{a}||\boldsymbol{b}| \sin \theta $ on the LHS

(M1)

$$ \left|\boldsymbol{a}\times\boldsymbol{b}\right|^{2}=\left|\boldsymbol{a}\right|^{2}\left|\boldsymbol{b}\right|^{2}\sin^{2}\theta $$

A1

$$ =\left|\boldsymbol{a}\right|^{2}\left|\boldsymbol{b}\right|^{2}\left(1-\cos^{2}\theta\right) $$

M1

$$ =\left|\boldsymbol{a}\right|^{2}\left|\boldsymbol{b}\right|^{2}-\left|\boldsymbol{a}\right|^{2}\left|\boldsymbol{b}\right|^{2}\cos^{2}\theta\text{OR}=\left|\boldsymbol{a}\right|^{2}\left|\boldsymbol{b}\right|^{2}-\left(\left|\boldsymbol{a}\right|\left|\boldsymbol{b}\right|\cos\theta\right)^{2} $$

A1

$$ \left|a\right|^{2}\left|b\right|^{2}-\left(a\cdot b\right)^{2} $$

AG

## ## METHOD 2

use of  $ a \cdot b = |a||b|\cos\theta $ on the RHS

(M1)

$$ =\left|\boldsymbol{a}\right|^{2}\left|\boldsymbol{b}\right|^{2}-\left|\boldsymbol{a}\right|^{2}\left|\boldsymbol{b}\right|^{2}\cos^{2}\theta $$

A1

$$ =|\boldsymbol{a}|^{2}\left|\boldsymbol{b}\right|^{2}\left(1-\cos^{2}\theta\right) $$

M1

$$ =\left|\boldsymbol{a}\right|^{2}\left|\boldsymbol{b}\right|^{2}\sin^{2}\theta\text{OR}=\left(\left|\boldsymbol{a}\right|\left|\boldsymbol{b}\right|\sin\theta\right)^{2} $$

A1

$$ =|a\times b|^{2} $$

AG
