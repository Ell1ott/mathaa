## ## EITHER

$$ \mathrm{volume~of~part~sphere}=\pi\int\left(r^{2}-y^{2}\right)\mathrm{d}y $$

A1

correct integration

$$ r^{2}y-\frac{y^{3}}{3} $$

attempt to substitute their limits into their integrated expression

(M1)

$$ \frac{r^{2}h}{2}-\frac{h^{3}}{24} $$

recognition that the volume of the ring is  $ \pi\int\left(r^{2}-y^{2}\right)\mathrm{d}y-\pi R^{2}h $ where  $ R\neq r $

(M1)

$$ \pi\int\left(r^{2}-y^{2}\right)\mathrm{d}y-\pi\left(r^{2}-\frac{h^{2}}{4}\right)h\text{(or equivalent)} $$

(A1)

correct equation

$$ 2\pi\left(\frac{r^{2}h}{2}-\frac{h^{3}}{24}\right)-\pi r^{2}h+\frac{\pi h^{3}}{4}=\pi\quad OR\quad\frac{h^{3}}{4}-\frac{h^{3}}{12}=1\quad(or~equivalent) $$

OR

$$  recognition~that~the~volume~of~the~ring~is~\pi\int\left(\left(r^{2}-y^{2}\right)-\left(r^{2}-\frac{h^{2}}{4}\right)\right)\mathrm{d}y~(or~equivalent) $$

(M1)

correct integration

A1

$$ \frac{h^{2}}{4}y-\frac{y^{3}}{3} $$

attempt to substitute their limits into their integrated expression

(M1)

$$ \frac{h^{3}}{8}-\frac{h^{3}}{24} $$

correct equation

(A1)

$$ 2\pi\left(\frac{h^{3}}{8}-\frac{h^{3}}{24}\right)=\pi\mathrm{OR}\quad2\left(\frac{h^{3}}{8}-\frac{h^{3}}{24}\right)=1\mathrm{(or~equivalent)} $$

THEN

$$ h=\sqrt[3]{6} $$

## ## METHOD 2 (volume of cylindrical hole)

$$  radius~of~cylinder,~R~~is~\sqrt{r^{2}-\frac{h^{2}}{4}}\quad OR\quad R^{2}=r^{2}-\frac{h^{2}}{4}\quad(seen~anywhere) $$

(A1)

$$ \mathrm{c o r r e c t~l i m i t s}\;\frac{h}{2}\;\mathrm{a n d}\;\;r\;\mathrm{(s e e n~a n y w h e r e)} $$

(A1)

$$ \mathrm{volume~of~part~sphere}=\pi\int\left(r^{2}-y^{2}\right)\mathrm{d}y $$

correct integration

A1

$$ r^{2}y-\frac{y^{3}}{3} $$

attempt to substitute their limits into their integrated expression

(M1)

$$ \frac{2r^{3}}{3}-\frac{r^{2}h}{2}+\frac{h^{3}}{24} $$

recognition that the volume of the cylindrical hole is  $ \pi\int\left(r^{2}-y^{2}\right)\mathrm{d}y+\pi R^{2}h $ where  $ R\neq r $

(M1)

$$ \pi\int\left(r^{2}-y^{2}\right)\mathrm{d}y+\pi\left(r^{2}-\frac{h^{2}}{4}\right)h\ \left(=\frac{4}{3}\pi r^{3}-\pi\right)(or~equivalent) $$

correct equation

(A1)

$$ 2\pi\left(\frac{2r^{3}}{3}-\frac{r^{2}h}{2}+\frac{h^{3}}{24}\right)+\pi r^{2}h-\frac{\pi h^{3}}{4}=\frac{4}{3}\pi r^{3}-\pi\mathrm{~O R~}\frac{h^{3}}{12}-\frac{h^{3}}{4}=-1\mathrm{~(o r~e q u i v a l e n t)} $$

$$ h=\sqrt[3]{6} $$

A1

## ## METHOD 3 (shells)

$$  radius~of~cylinder,~R~~is~\sqrt{r^{2}-\frac{h^{2}}{4}}~~OR~~R^{2}=r^{2}-\frac{h^{2}}{4}\quad(seen~anywhere) $$

(A1)

attempt to use shells method

(M1)

$$ 2\pi\int x\sqrt{r^{2}-x^{2}}\mathrm{d}x $$

$$ \mathrm{c o r r e c t~l i m i t s}~r\mathrm{~a n d~}\sqrt{r^{2}-\frac{h^{2}}{4}}\mathrm{~(s e e n~a n y w h e r e)} $$

(A1)

correct integration

A1

$$ -\frac{1}{3}\Big(r^{2}-x^{2}\Big)^{\frac{3}{2}} $$

attempt to substitute their limits into their integrated expression

(M1)

$$ -\frac{1}{3}\left(0-\left(r^{2}-\left(r^{2}-\frac{h^{2}}{4}\right)\right)^{\frac{3}{2}}\right) $$

correct equation

(A1)

$$ 2\times\frac{-2\pi}{3}\left(0-\left(r^{2}-\left(r^{2}-\frac{h^{2}}{4}\right)\right)^{\frac{3}{2}}\right)=\pi\quad\mathrm{OR}\quad2\left(\frac{2\pi}{3}\times\frac{h^{3}}{8}\right)=\pi $$

$$ h=\sqrt[3]{6} $$
