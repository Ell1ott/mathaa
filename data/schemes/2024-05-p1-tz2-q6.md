(A1)

recognition of conditional probability in context

(M1)

$$ \mathrm{P}(\mathrm{eating~apple}|\mathrm{weight}>140)=\frac{\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{eating~apple~and~weight~of~eating~apple}>140)}{\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{weight~of~apple}>140)} $$

OR

$$ \mathrm{P(eating~apple~\cap~weight~of~eating~apple>140)} $$

P(eating apple  $ \cap $ weight of eating apple >140) + P(cooking apple  $ \cap $ weight of cooking apple >140)

OR

$$ \mathrm{P}(\mathrm{eating~apple})\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{weight~of~eating~apple}>140) $$

$$ \begin{aligned}P(eating~apple)&P(weight~of~eating~apple>140)+P(cooking~apple)P(weight~of~cooking~apple>140)\\ &\\&=\frac{0.8\times0.025}{0.8\times0.025+0.2\times0.5}\left(=\frac{80\times2.5}{80\times2.5+20\times50}\right)\tag{A1}\\ &\\&=\frac{200}{1200}\left(=\frac{1}{6}\right)\tag{A2}\\ \end{aligned} $$
