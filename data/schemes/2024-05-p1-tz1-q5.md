## ## METHOD 3

let P(n) be the proposition that $S_{1}+S_{2}+S_{3}+\ldots+S_{n}=\frac{10(10^{n}-1)-9n}{81}$

considering P(1):

$$  LHS=S_{1}=\frac{10^{1}-1}{9}=1\ and\ RHS=\frac{10\left(10^{1}-1\right)-9(1)}{81}=1\ and so\ P(1)\ is true $$

R1

$$  assume\mathbf{P}(k)is true i.e.S_{1}+S_{2}+S_{3}+\ldots+S_{k}=\frac{10\Big(10^{k}-1\Big)-9k}{81} $$

M1
