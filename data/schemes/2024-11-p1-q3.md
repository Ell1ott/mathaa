## ## METHOD 3

base case n=1:  $ (3(1)+2)^2-\left(3(1)-2\right)^2=25-1=24 $

so true for n=1

A1

assume true for $n = k$ i.e. $\left(3k + 2\right)^{2} - \left(3k - 2\right)^{2}$ is a multiple of 12

M1

consider n = k + 1:

$$ \left(3(k+1)+2\right)^{2}-\left(3(k+1)-2\right)^{2} $$

$$ \left(\left(3k+2\right)+3\right)^{2}-\left(\left(3k-2\right)+3\right)^{2} $$

$$ \left(3k+2\right)^{2}-\left(3k-2\right)^{2}+24 $$

using the assumption  $  (3k+2)^2 - (3k-2)^2 = 12M  $

12M+24

$$ 12\left(M+2\right) $$

which is a multiple of 12, hence true for n = k + 1
